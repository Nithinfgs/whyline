# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, welcome newcomers, accept constructive criticism, focus on what is best for the project and its users, and do not harass or demean anyone. Public or private harassment, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this, and may ban contributors who do so repeatedly.

To report a problem, use GitHub's [private reporting](https://github.com/Nithinfgs/whyline/security/advisories/new) or contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Nithinfgs). Reports are handled confidentially.
